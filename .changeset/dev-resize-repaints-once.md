Resizing the window while `pithy dev` runs no longer stacks stale roster headers on the screen. During a drag ink's resize repaint is muted and the roster is erased on every event; the clock tick and output committed mid-drag still paint, into a screen the erases keep clean. Once the size has held for 150 ms the roster is drawn once, at the final width, and a drag that crossed more than one size rebuilds the visible screen under it: your output above the roster is restated in place at the new width — counted in rows (a 150-column line is three rows at 60, the roster's own lines are one row each) — and each restated line runs through the same ANSI sanitizer ink applies to text on the normal path, so colors and hyperlinks survive and a worker's stray erase or cursor move never reaches the terminal. Lines that had scrolled up keep their single copy in scrollback; a repaint racing ConPTY's reflow can leave at most three copies of a visible-tail line, the bound the Windows suite pins. The rebuild's erase covers the visible screen, so the first drag in a fresh terminal erases the shell output on screen above `pithy dev`'s first line; it does not return to scrollback.
