Find competitors. Room codes, invites, friends, and matched queues — every path lands players in an authoritative multiplayer session. Room codes are short, shareable, and limited-use; invites go by email or screen name; friends form a symmetric graph; the open queue pairs players in a per-game Durable Object, bucketed by region and skill, widening the band the longer they wait. A presence Durable Object pushes pending invites, online friends, and "match found."
